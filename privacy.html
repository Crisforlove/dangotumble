简体中文

我们不收集任何个人数据。

《团子翻车》不需要注册或登录，不包含广告，不包含第三方统计或追踪代码，也不会把任何信息发送到我们的服务器。

保存在你设备上的内容：最高分、每日挑战的当日记录、已合成过的团子、所选主题。这些只存在你的设备里，删除 App 时一并清除。

相册权限：只有当你主动选择把回放“保存视频”时，系统才会请求向相册添加内容的权限。App 不会读取你的相册。

分享：回放视频和成绩卡片在你的设备上生成。只有你主动点分享并选择对象时，它们才会通过系统分享功能发出。

儿童：本游戏不收集任何人的数据，包括儿童。

变更：如果将来加入广告、排行榜或其他会处理数据的功能，我们会先更新本页面和 App Store 上的隐私说明。

English

We do not collect any personal data.

Dango Tumble needs no account or sign-in. It contains no ads and no third-party analytics or tracking code, and it does not send any information to our servers.

Stored on your device: your best score, today's daily challenge record, the dango you have discovered, and your chosen theme. This stays on your device and is removed when you delete the app.

Photos permission: the system asks for permission to add to your photo library only when you choose “Save Video” for a replay. The app never reads your photos.

Sharing: replay videos and score cards are created on your device. They leave it only when you tap share and pick a destination in the system share sheet.

Children: the game collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes: if ads, leaderboards or other features that process data are added later, this page and the App Store privacy details will be updated first.
