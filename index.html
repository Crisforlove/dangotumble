怎么玩

按住屏幕左右移动，松手让团子落下。两个相同的团子碰到一起会合成更大的。托盘会随重量倾斜，团子滚出去就少一颗心，三颗心用完游戏结束。

常见问题

猫咪团子主题怎么解锁？
合成出一次团子王之后自动解锁，在首页左上角的调色盘里切换。

每日挑战是什么？
每天所有玩家拿到同一串团子，日期按世界协调时（UTC）切换。

我的记录会丢吗？
分数和图鉴只保存在这台设备上。删除 App 会一并清除。

How to play

Drag left or right, then release to drop a dango. Two of the same kind merge into a bigger one. The tray tilts with the weight on it. Every dango that rolls off costs a heart, and the game ends when all three are gone.

FAQ

How do I unlock the Cat Dango theme?
It unlocks the first time you make the Dango King. Switch themes with the palette button at the top left of the home screen.

What is the daily challenge?
Every player gets the same sequence of dango each day. The day changes at midnight UTC.

Can I lose my progress?
Scores and your collection are stored only on this device. Deleting the app removes them.
